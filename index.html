Pookkottumala Model

Pookkottumala achieved sustainable development with people participation.

Activities: food self-sufficiency · watersheds · greenery · arid-free · Jalasamridhi · filament-free village

Supported by local self-government institutions and active grama sabhas.

Grama Sabha

Meeting of all adult voters of a ward/village. Plans development, identifies beneficiaries, monitors works.

People participation + planning + officers' support = progress

Local Self-Government

Grama panchayat, block panchayat, district panchayat (rural); municipality / corporation (urban).

They plan and implement local development with people involvement.

Gandhiji's Grama Swaraj

Village as a complete republic — self-sufficient in food and cloth, yet interdependent where needed.

Soul of India lies in the villages · Progress through village development

Decentralisation

Transfer of power from higher levels to local bodies and people.

Means to realise grama swaraj. When power reaches the people, welfare increases.

Sustainable Development

Meeting present needs without harming future generations.

Examples in Pookkottumala: greenery, watershed, food self-sufficiency, waste-free (garbage-free) wards.
